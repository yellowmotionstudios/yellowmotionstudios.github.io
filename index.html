Yellow Motion Studios Uploader

Yellow Motion Studios Uploader is the studio's own production tool. The studio uses it only to upload its own finished videos to its own YouTube channels, with their title, description and tags, through the YouTube Data API.

It is not offered to the public, it does not access anyone else's account, and it does not collect data from viewers. Details are in the privacy policy and terms.
