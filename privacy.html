Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

Who we are

Yellow Motion Studios Uploader ("the app") is a private production tool of Yellow Motion Studios. The studio uses it only to upload its own videos to its own YouTube channels (currently The Airway Archive). It is not offered to other users.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Information handled through YouTube is also subject to the Google Privacy Policy.

What information the app accesses

With the permission of the studio's own Google account for each channel (Google sign-in), the app uses two permissions:

  • Upload videos (youtube.upload): to upload the studio's finished videos with their title, description, tags, category and privacy setting.
  • View the YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to read the channel's name and the status of the videos it uploaded (for example, whether processing has finished and whether a video is private or public).

The app does not access viewers' data, comments, subscribers, or any other account.

How the information is used

Only to upload and confirm the studio's own videos. Nothing is used for advertising, profiling or analytics, and nothing is sold.

Storage

The sign-in token (a Google refresh token) is stored only on the studio's own production computer, in a private folder, and is never shared. Upload records (video ID, link, privacy status, date) are kept on that computer for the studio's production log. The app has no server and keeps no other copies.

Sharing

Information is not shared with anyone. Videos and their details are sent only to YouTube, as part of uploading them.

Cookies

The app does not use cookies or tracking. This website does not use cookies, analytics or trackers.

How to remove access

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. When access is revoked, the stored token stops working and is deleted from the studio's computer. To have any stored data deleted, contact us at the address below; we delete it within 7 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date.
